A Dictionary of Galactic Extinction

A Dictionary of Galactic Extinction began with a simple question: what would happen if light could be trapped inside a perfectly mirrored room and never escape? From that question grew a fragmented history of light, energy, civilization, and extinction — told through physics, records, people, words, and documents rather than a single protagonist or plot.

Sweater

Sweater follows a fourteen-year-old boy through a year of school, family, friendship, first love, and small discoveries that shape his life. Told as a diary, the novel moves through everyday events as the boy gradually comes to understand himself, others, and the world around him. Its voice is curious, restless, comic, and reflective — capturing both the energy and quiet observations of adolescence.

Music

The music here grew out of scenes, emotions, and lines that came to mind while I was writing fiction. The lyrics, musical direction, mood, and overall structure were created and directed by Jin-in-Seoul, while the music itself was generated using Suno AI. Some of these pieces began as things that never found their way into the novels, or as ideas that seemed to belong more naturally in music than in sentences.

Writer

Jin-in-Seoul studied Philosophy and Journalism at Hankuk University of Foreign Studies. He received an award in the Essay category at the 2015 Esquire Montblanc Literary Award. His novel Sweater was published by Byeolsoop in 2018.
