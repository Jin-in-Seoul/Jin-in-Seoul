Management & Maintenance

Jin-in-Seoul preserves literary works and their translations as independent HTML files. Its management documents record the principles of preservation, editing, translation, publication and technical maintenance, so that the works and the operation of the site remain independent of any particular software or administrator.

The seven common management documents below are rendered directly from their official Markdown sources in the GitHub repository, in English. Work-specific translation guides are retained in the repository for future translations.

Contents

  2. README — Management Overview
  3. CSS Maintenance
  4. SEO Maintenance
  5. Asset Maintenance
