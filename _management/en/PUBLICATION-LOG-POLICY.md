# Publication Log Policy — Jin-in-Seoul

Repository: `Jin-in-Seoul/Jin-in-Seoul` (`main`).
Public log: https://jin-in-seoul.com/publication-log/
Source file: `publication-log/index.html`.

## 1. Purpose and authority

The Publication Log is a chronological historical record of releases, translations, chapter additions, and significant publication-related changes on Jin-in-Seoul.

This document establishes the rules for maintaining that record consistently over time. It governs the Publication Log only; it does not change the names, wording, translation, or publication dates on individual literary pages. The author has final editorial authority.

## 2. Record language and work titles

- Log dates, headings, prose and descriptions are written in English.
- Each work's established English title serves as its common identifier, including when recording Japanese, French, and Korean editions.
- English titles are verified against the live English edition, listing or other approved source before entry. New official titles are not established by guesswork or retranslation.
- Where a section heading already identifies the work in English, the language-specific entry may simply say "Japanese edition — Chapters 1–4 added" without repeating its title.
- Groups of short stories use the same approved English titles in every language entry.
- Appendix titles in the Publication Log use the established official English title, regardless of edition language. The applicable title is **Appendix — An Index for Those Lost in the Galaxy** for the corresponding Korean and English editions of *A Dictionary of Galactic Extinction*. The edition's own published table of contents may retain its language-specific title. Invented translations and confusion between this common title and individual edition dates or language identities are excluded.
- **Exceptions:** Haiku may retain Japanese originals with English titles; Music may retain the existing English/Korean/Japanese bilingual or multilingual song-title presentation. Standardization of those titles requires author approval.

## 3. Language order

When editions or languages share the same date and priority, the following order applies:

**English → Japanese → French → Korean**

- This order applies both to language lists within a sentence and to individual edition entries grouped under the same work and date.
- When only some languages are present, their relative order follows that sequence.
- Separate historical dates retain their order regardless of language priority.
- Publication is not inferred from an absent release record.

## 4. Actual publication dates and history

- Each edition is recorded on the **date it was actually published**, not the date of the original work or another translation.
- If the first editions were released on one day and later translations on another, separate dated entries are maintained. For instance, the English and Korean editions of Gray Note 008 were logged on November 10, 2026, while the Japanese and French editions were logged on November 11, 2026.
- Language releases are not backdated or combined merely to form a four-language entry.
- Historical log entries describe what was known, announced, or released **at that time**. Past plans and publication milestones remain historical records even when plans change; later decisions are documented under their own dates.
- Dates, edition languages, chapter/part numbers, release sequence, PDF/EPUB details and the distinction between releases and maintenance events are preserved.
- Corrections to factual errors may be made with the author's approval and should not invent a new publication event.

## 5. Event wording

The following terms correspond to the event that actually occurred:

- **added:** a newly published work, chapter, language edition, or downloadable edition.
- **updated:** a maintenance or technical change to an existing published file or page without necessarily changing the literary text.
- **revised:** an authorized substantive revision of published literary text.

Neutral release wording is preferred, such as **English edition added** or **French edition added**. Publication Log entries exclude **complete**, **completed**, **full text published**, and similar claims of finality; a work or edition may be revised after release. Actual chapter numbers, collection size and publication events are preserved without declaring a work permanently finished. The policy prohibits relabelling an update as a literary revision.

## 6. Titles, subtitles, and consistency

- The same approved English title applies wherever a work recurs in the log.
- Where a particular log entry includes the work's subtitle, the same approved subtitle applies to later dated entries referring to that release when relevant. A later language release alone does not justify silently dropping a subtitle.
- New releases require consistency verification between the English page title and subtitle, index/listing and proposed log entry, rather than conjecture.
- Original wording in Haiku and Music is preserved under their exceptions.

## 7. Editing and publishing procedure

1. The procedure begins with review of the latest `main` versions of this policy, `publication-log/index.html` and relevant work pages.
2. The actual release date, languages, approved English title, subtitle if used and event type are confirmed.
3. The appropriate dated section is drafted or updated in chronological order, with entries grouped by work and languages listed in the fixed order.
4. Each language edition or chapter is verified against actual publication; unrelated historical entries remain unchanged.
5. Review alone does not authorize GitHub changes. Changes require explicit authorization.
6. Authorized changes to `main` require the latest file SHA and preservation of intervening changes.
7. Post-commit verification includes retrieval of the file and confirmation of text, dates, language order, titles, subtitle consistency and preservation of previous records.
8. Verification includes GitHub Pages deployment and applicable repository audits. Source commits and completed live deployment are recorded separately.

## 8. Scope and safeguards

- This policy does not authorize edits to the underlying literary works, translations, original photographs, audio, PDF/EPUB files, site layouts, or unrelated navigation.
- Historical formatting cleanup must not change a release's meaning or its actual date.
- Uncertain English translations of formerly non-English-only titles require verification or author approval, not invented equivalents.
- Apparent inconsistencies require review of actual source pages and publication history before either entry is changed.
- The author may revise this policy; changes should be made explicitly and documented in the repository.
