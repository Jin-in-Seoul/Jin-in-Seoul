# Work-specific Translation Guide — The Tale of Jeon Unchi

The shared translation and editing principles and English glossary follow the [shared translation guide](PREMODERN-KOREAN-FICTION-TRANSLATION-GUIDE.md).

## The Tale of Jeon Unchi

### 1. Edition and Names (Established)

Use the established modern Korean edition based on the 37-leaf Gyeongpan edition of 1847, 『뎐운치젼』. Distinguish the widely used title 『전우치전』 from the particular edition's title 『전운치전』. The English title is *The Tale of Jeon Unchi*, and the protagonist is Jeon Unchi / Unchi. Do not change these to Jeon Uchi / Uchi in the text. Preserve this distinction in the edition note. Romanise the edition titles as Jeon Unchi-jeon (전운치전) and Jeon Uchi-jeon (전우치전).

### 4. Narrative Voice and Trial Translation

The title *The Tale of* alone can evoke the opening of an old tale. Do not artificially archaise the body text.

Example opening sentence (place name not yet established):
*Towards the end of the Goryeo dynasty, there lived a renowned scholar in Namseobu. His surname was Jeon, his given name was Suk, and he was known as Master Unhwa.*

In dialogue involving a young lady (낭자), the politeness of a question such as *May I ask where you live?* can convey the form of address. This is an example of handling address, not permission to omit the full original dialogue.

Style example for the hanging-scroll scene in Chapter 8:
*Lady Min raised her hand to strike the celestial maiden, but the maiden was already a painting again.*
Preserve the moment when the result of the magical art is first discovered. Do not invent actions such as disappearing in a flash of light or withdrawing into the painting. This is an example proposed during strategy discussions and is established only after comparison with the actual source.

Trial translation covers narration and dialogue between parents, teachers, men and women in Chapter 1, The Fox's Bead; court dialogue in Chapter 7, The False Accusation of Treason; and magical arts and humour in Chapter 8, The Woman in the Painting. Review these three passages before establishing tone and terminology for the rest.

### 5. Translation and Review Procedure

1. Check the established Korean edition and glossary in the shared translation guide.
2. Verify unestablished work-specific terms and place names, and conduct trial translation.
3. Present review findings and obtain approval before establishing terminology and style.
4. Translate while preserving the numbered chapter titles and paragraph structure of Chapters 1–10.
5. Compare paragraph by paragraph with the Korean edition to identify omissions, additions, mistranslations and inconsistent names.
6. Apply corrections after approval and record new decisions in the shared glossary.

### 6. English Revision Decisions

The accepted revisions above were agreed during individual reviews of Chapters 1–10. Finalise them after comparison of the complete translation with the source and verification of paragraph boundaries. The geographical nature of Namseobu remains unresolved.
