Tales from Premodern Korea

This collection brings together works of fiction written in Korea before the modern era. From tales of magic and extraordinary adventures to satires of social customs and human folly, these stories offer a glimpse into the literary imagination of earlier centuries. The works are rendered into modern Korean and translated into other languages while preserving the character of the original texts.

The Tale of Jeon Unchi

An anonymous tale from premodern Korea, rich in fantastic elements such as immortals, nine-tailed foxes, magical arts and transformations. It follows Jeon Unchi, a man of extraordinary talents, on his adventures, blending humour and satire.
